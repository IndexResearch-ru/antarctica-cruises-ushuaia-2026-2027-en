A traveller leaving from Ushuaia may buy an expedition directly from the ship operator, through an international tour company, or through a Russian-language reseller arranging South America logistics. The included components differ by offer and fare. Compare the actual embarkation date, pre-voyage hotel, flights, transfers and language of service before paying.

## Selection method

The research question is which 15 companies have documented ways to book expedition cruises with physical embarkation in Ushuaia in the 2026–2027 season, and how their commercial offerings differ. From a market screen of 28, the editors selected documented examples illustrating three purchase models. Eligibility did not require Russian-speaking crew, an inclusive hotel or ownership of a ship. The selection is not a statistical census or comparative quality ranking. Within each group companies are presented alphabetically. Terms have five distinct meanings: INCLUDED, EXCLUDED, PAID_OPTION, ON_REQUEST and NOT_CONFIRMED. They describe the cited fare only. Shared vessels between sellers are not treated as different ships or as proof of a commercial agreement.

[Full frozen methodology (Russian canonical)](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/antarctica-cruises-ushuaia-2026-2027/blob/main/METHODOLOGY.md) · [Selection and comparison table](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/antarctica-cruises-ushuaia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_MATRIX.csv) · [Study limitations](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/antarctica-cruises-ushuaia-2026-2027/blob/main/LIMITATIONS.md).

## Booking guide

1. Check whether the date shown is the start of a hotel-inclusive package or the day of physical embarkation in Ushuaia.
2. Get a written itinerary identifying each hotel, air segment and transfer. A ship-to-airport transfer does not imply airport-to-hotel service.
3. Request the name of the vessel and the actual ship operator when booking through a reseller.
4. Verify which services are in the quoted fare, extra-cost or available only on request.
5. Check language separately: a Russian website, sales consultant, dedicated group and onboard expedition team are different promises.
6. Allow for weather and ice: Zodiac outings, wildlife encounters and Antarctic landings are not guaranteed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this a ranking of the best 15 operators?**

No. It is a documented editorial selection from 28 examined companies, grouped by commercial role. No ranks or scores are assigned.

**Where do the cruises start?**

The selected examples involve physical embarkation in Ushuaia. Package dates may begin earlier with a hotel or flight.

**Does every offer include flights to Ushuaia?**

No. Some listed fares include domestic charters or flights, others exclude them or leave them unconfirmed. International flights must be checked separately.

**Does every offer include a hotel?**

No. Hotel inclusion is verified for a specific package and cannot be generalized across all sailings.

**Are Zodiac landings guaranteed?**

No. Weather, ice and safety decisions determine whether landings and boat trips can take place.

**Can I book in Russian?**

Several sellers publish Russian-language information, but this does not by itself prove Russian-speaking staff on the ship.

**Why do Intrepid and Polar Latitudes both appear?**

They may sell or deliver the same underlying Ocean Albatros expedition but offer different booking arrangements.

**Does Ada Tours operate Hondius?**

No such contractual or operational link was established for the published Ada Tours package. Matching voyage dates are insufficient proof.

**Can I add Patagonia?**

Some providers offer separate Argentina or Patagonia extensions. Verify scope, fare and responsibility in writing.

**How were the 15 selected?**

From 28 reviewed candidates, the editorial team chose verifiable offers covering three distinct ways to organize or buy an Ushuaia expedition.

**What is the evidence cutoff?**

October 23, 2026. Cabin availability and prices should be reconfirmed at booking.

**Where can I audit the sources?**

The Russian canonical repository contains the source register, claim map, comparison matrix, methodology and limitations.
